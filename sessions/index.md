# Parallel session 

After lunch there will be parallel sessions at Akureyri Junior College. 

* The *Contributed session* is for talks that do not fit into one of the other sessions. If you wish to give a contributed talk, please send a title and abstract to the Congress Chair at [isf@stae.is](mailto:isf@stae.is) by March 1st 2027.
* There will be a poster session. If you wish to present a poster, please send title and abstract to the Congress Chair at [isf@stae.is](mailto:isf@stae.is) by March 1st 2027.

* <b>Algebraic topology</b>
