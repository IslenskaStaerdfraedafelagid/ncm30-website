# Parallel session 

After lunch there will be parallel sessions at Akureyri Junior College. 

The *Contributed session* is for talks that do not fit into one of the other sessions.
There will also be a poster session.
If you wish to give a contributed talk or if you wish to present a poster,
  please send a title and abstract to the Congress Chair at [isf@stae.is](mailto:isf@stae.is) by March 1st 2027.
